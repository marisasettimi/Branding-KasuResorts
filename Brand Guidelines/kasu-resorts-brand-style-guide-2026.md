# Kasu Resorts - Brand Style Guide

> Where extraordinary places meet intentional design.

---

## 1. Brand Overview

Kasu Resorts is a luxury hospitality brand rooted in the belief that every destination tells a story. Our brand identity balances sophistication with warmth, structure with nature, and precision with emotion.

**Mission:** To create extraordinary resort experiences that connect guests to the beauty of place through considered design and genuine hospitality.

**Voice:** Confident, warm, elevated yet approachable. We speak like a trusted concierge - knowledgeable, never pretentious.

---

## 2. Logo System

### Primary Logo

The Kasu wordmark uses **Neue Haas Grotesk** at 600 weight with generous letter-spacing (0.12em). Always maintain minimum clear space equal to the cap height of the "K".

### Brand Symbol

The Kasu icon (chevron + bar) can be used independently at small sizes such as favicons, app icons, and social avatars. Never rotate, distort, or recolor outside the approved palette.

### Minimum Sizes

| Format | Minimum Width |
|--------|--------------|
| Full logo | 120px |
| Icon only | 24px |
| Print logo | 35mm |
| Print icon | 8mm |

### Don'ts

- Never place the logo on busy photography without a container or overlay.
- Never use drop shadows or outlines on the logo.
- Never stretch, compress, or rotate the logo.
- Never change individual letter colors.

## 3. Color Palette

A Blue Sky hospitality palette. The gradient flows from sky to clouds to sunlight, with blues used sparingly as accents.

### Core Palette

| Color | Hex | Ratio | Usage |
|-------|-----|-------|-------|
| Cloud | `#E9EDF0` | 55% | Main page background. Bright, soft, breathable |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | 28% | Cards, sections, content areas |
| Deep Sky | `#475C6B` | 10% | Primary brand color. Buttons, links, key accents |
| Mid Sky | `#6E8FA8` | 4% | Hover states, subtle highlights, small UI elements |
| Graphite | `#2F2F2F` | 3% | Text, headings, icons. Avoid pure black |
| Horizon Haze | `#A9BCC7` | as needed | Cards, dividers, muted interface elements |
| Sunlit Sand | `#F3EFE4` | accent | Warm touches on white backgrounds only. Never as a background |

### Functional

| Color | Hex | Usage |
|-------|-----|-------|
| Success | `#4A6741` | Positive indicators |
| Warning | `#B8860B` | Caution states |
| Error | `#8B3A3A` | Error states, alerts |

### Accessibility

All text and background combinations must meet **WCAG AA**: 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text. Graphite text on Cloud and Sunlit Sand backgrounds passes. Deep Sky on Cloud passes for large text. Let photography and space dominate rather than heavy color blocks.

| Tag | Neue Haas Grotesk | 500 | 11px | 0.08em |
| Quote | EB Garamond | 400 italic | 28px | 0 |
| Numbers | EB Garamond | 400 | varies | 0 |

### Hierarchy Rules

- Neue Haas Grotesk is the primary typeface for headlines, body, UI, and labels.
- EB Garamond is used for numbers, statistics, quotes, testimonials, and editorial italic moments.
- Weight variation from 200-700 within Neue Haas Grotesk creates the structural hierarchy.

### Line Heights

- Headings: 1.1-1.2
- Body: 1.6-1.7
- Captions: 1.4

## 5. Photography Direction

### Style

Natural light, warm tones, editorial composition. Capture the feeling of a place - not just the space. Human presence should feel organic, never staged.

Prioritize four shot families: aerial overviews, warm interiors, amenity compositions, and detail-led room moments.

### Treatment

- Subtle warm grade with lifted shadows and reduced highlights.
- Muted saturation - never over-processed.
- Prefer golden hour, blue hour, or soft overcast light.
- Aspect ratios: 16:9 for hero, 4:5 for social, and 1:1 for grid.

### Don'ts

- No heavy filters or visible vignettes.
- No stock photography cliches such as handshakes or people pointing at screens.
- No oversaturated skies or HDR effects.

## 6. Voice Guidelines

### Writing Principles

1. **Lead with specificity** - "Wake to the sound of waves on volcanic rock" over "Enjoy your stay".
2. **Be warm, not casual** - professional yet personal.
3. **Show, don't tell** - evoke experiences rather than listing features.
4. **Use active voice** - "Discover hidden coves" over "Hidden coves can be discovered".

### Capitalization

- Headlines: Title Case
- Body: Sentence case
- CTAs: Sentence case
- Navigation: Title Case

### Punctuation

- Use hyphens or short dashes for parenthetical thoughts.
- Oxford comma: always.
- Periods in headlines: no.
- Exclamation marks: avoid.

## 7. Icon System

### Style

- 1.5px stroke weight matching body text weight.
- Rounded line caps.
- 24x24px grid with 2px padding.
- Single color that inherits the current text color.

### Usage

- Navigation: 20x20px
- Inline with text: match x-height
- Feature grids: 32x32px or 48x48px
- Always maintain 8px minimum clearance from text

---

## 8. Spacing & Layout

### Base Unit: 8px

All spacing uses multiples of 8px. Common values:

- `8px` - tight, for icon to label spacing
- `16px` - default, for related items
- `24px` - comfortable, for section padding
- `32px` - section breaks
- `48px` - major divisions
- `80px` - panel covers

### Grid

- Max content width: 1200px
- Column gap: 24px
- Common grids: 2-column, 3-column, and 4-column
- Mobile breakpoint: 768px, collapsing to a single column

---

## 9. Components

### Buttons

- Border radius: 6px
- Padding: 12px 24px
- Font: Neue Haas Grotesk, 13px, 500 weight
- Primary: Deep Sky background with white text
- Secondary: transparent with a 1px dark border

### Cards

- Border radius: 12px
- Background: Cloud or White
- Padding: 32px
- Subtle border: 1px solid rgba(0,0,0,0.06)

### Tags / Badges

- Font: Neue Haas Grotesk, 11px, 500 weight, uppercase, 0.08em tracking
- Padding: 4px 10px
- Border radius: 4px

## 10. Digital Applications

### Website

- Hero sections: full-bleed photography with text overlay
- Navigation: minimal and sticky
- Booking flow: clean, step-by-step, and low-friction

### Email

- Max width: 600px
- Header: Kasu icon plus wordmark
- Body: Neue Haas Grotesk with Helvetica Neue fallback
- CTA buttons: Deep Sky with 6px radius

### Social Media

- Maintain brand colors in graphics.
- Photography-first approach.
- Design native formats for Instagram, Pinterest, TikTok, YouTube, X, Reddit, LinkedIn, and Facebook.
- Match the channel behavior: save-worthy Pins, vertical short-form, concise conversation graphics, and clean editorial stat cards.
- Use the correct platform marks and keep social icons visually consistent at small sizes.
- Consistent use of brand typefaces in overlays.
- Branded hashtags: `#KasuResorts`, `#StayKasu`, `#KasuExperience`, `#CuratedStays`
